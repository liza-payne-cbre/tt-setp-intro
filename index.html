What we do

When a growing technology company needs a new lab, a test range, a clean room, a hangar or a manufacturing line, the hard part is rarely the idea. It is knowing what it will cost, when it will be ready and how to keep it on track once it starts. That is our job. We work alongside your team as an independent partner, from the first estimate through to handover.

Where we help

Cost management and estimating

Realistic budgets for labs, test facilities and capital programs, then tight cost control so there are no surprises at board level.

Project controls

Schedule, cost and risk baselines you can actually steer by, with reporting your leadership team can read in five minutes.

Project and program advisory

PMO set-up, governance and decision gates sized for your business, not borrowed from a company ten times your size.

Delivery support

Hands-on help delivering facilities and infrastructure expansions, including site selection, contractor procurement and fit-out.

Why it matters to a growing technology business

Your capital is finite and your timeline is not negotiable. A test facility that lands six months late or thirty percent over budget is not just a construction problem. It delays your product, your certification and your revenue.

Your team is built for engineering, not for construction. Independent cost and program management means you get seasoned delivery expertise without hiring a department you only need for eighteen months.

We have done this before. Turner & Townsend has deep experience across technology, aerospace and advanced manufacturing, from single-building fit-outs to multi-site global programs.
